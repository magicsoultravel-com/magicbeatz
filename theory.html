Theory is shortcuts, not rules

Use just enough theory to pick notes that fit, build chords fast, and steal progressions with confidence. If it sounds right, it is right - theory just explains why.

Chords in 30 seconds

Stack thirds. 1-3-5 = triad, add 7 for color.

Major
1 + M3 + P5 (C E G) - bright, resolved
Minor
1 + m3 + P5 (A C E) - sad, soft
Diminished
1 + m3 + d5 (B D F) - tense, wants to move
Augmented
1 + M3 + A5 (C E G#) - floating, dreamy
Sus2 / Sus4
Swap the 3rd for 2nd or 4th - open, hanging
7th
Add m7 for funk (C7), M7 for jazz (Cmaj7), m7 to minor for lo-fi (Am7)

Steal these progressions

I V vi IV - pop anthem (C G Am F). Works every time.
vi IV I V - sad-to-hopeful flip of the same four.
ii V I - jazz / neo-soul landing strip (Dm G C).
i VI III VII - minor epic (Am F C G). Trap + EDM staple.
I vi ii V - doo-wop / lo-fi loop (C Am Dm G).

Circle of fifths - pick keys that fit

Go clockwise = up a fifth (adds a sharp). Counterclockwise = down a fifth (adds a flat). Neighbors share almost all notes - safe to borrow. Opposite side = maximum drama.

C - Am (no sharps/flats)G - Em (1#)D - Bm (2#)F - Dm (1b)Bb - Gm (2b)Eb - Cm (3b)
Secondary dominant
Play the V of any chord before it (E7 before Am in key of C). Instant lift into choruses.
Chromatic mediant
Jump to a major/minor chord a third away with a shared note (C to Ab). Film-score drama.
Modulation
Move home base mid-song. Easiest: pivot on a chord both keys share, then land on the new V-I.
Harmonic rhythm
How fast chords change. Half-time feel = change every 2 bars. Hype = every beat.

Rhythm and form for beats

Top number = beats per bar. Bottom = which note gets one beat.

Simple meter
Beat splits in 2 (4/4, 3/4). Straight and driving.
Compound meter
Beat splits in 3 (6/8, 12/8). Swingy, rolling.
Asymmetric
Uneven bars like 5/4 or 7/8. Instant odd-loop character.

Arrange your loop

Binary AB - verse / chorus, done. Most beats live here.
Ternary ABA - loop, contrast, back home. Great for 2-min beats.
Rondo ABACA - hook keeps returning between verses.
Sonata - statement, chaos, bigger return. Think intro / build / drop.

Andante75-95walking boom-bap zone
Moderato95-115chill house, R&B
Allegro120-140house, pop, trap energy

Movers: accelerando = speed up gradually, ritardando / rallentando = slow down, a tempo = back to the original.

Loudness ladder

pp whisperp softmp medium-softmf medium-loudf loudff bangin

Crescendo (getting louder) into drops, decrescendo out of them. Sforzando = punch one note. Subito = sudden change.

Octaves, sound and ornaments

Name any note in any octave, then decorate it.

Pitch / Intensity
How high + how loud. Velocity = intensity on pads.
Duration / Timbre
How long + what color. Short plucks vs long pads.
Nonharmonic tones
Passing notes that are not in the chord - the spice between chord tones.

Ornaments in one line each

Passing / Neighbor - step through or around a note, then land home.
Escape / Appoggiatura - leap away, step back. Instant vocal run feel.
Suspension / Retardation - hold yesterday's note over today's chord. Sweet tension.
Anticipation / Changing tones - play the next chord early, or circle a note before landing.
